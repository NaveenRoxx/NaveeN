NEW IN LITE VERSION

Real-Time Performance Analytics

The latest update to the Lite version introduces a fully integrated analytics engine that tracks every aspect of the welding session — giving trainees and instructors actionable insight, not just a score.

  • Welding Speed Tracking — Detects if the torch is moved too fast or slow against the ideal range, correcting muscle memory in real time.
  • Arc Distance Monitoring — Measures torch-to-workpiece gap continuously, with live visual feedback when outside the safe zone.
  • Electrode Angle Analysis — Tracks tilt and travel angle throughout the bead, scoring adherence to correct posture.
  • Accuracy Score & Grade — Weighted composite score across all metrics, displayed as a grade and pass/fail status at session end.
  • Session Timestamp & Duration — Every session is logged with start time, duration, and completion status for record-keeping.
NEW IN LITE VERSION

Auto Report Generation

After every completed session, the simulator instantly generates a structured performance report and a personalised certificate — making it classroom-ready and institution-compliant out of the box.

  • Per-Metric Breakdown — Detailed scores for speed, arc distance, angle, and accuracy with visual pass/fail indicators per parameter.
  • Trainee Name & Roll Number — Reports are personalised with the trainee's identity, making them suitable for institutional records and evaluations.
  • Digital Certificate — Auto-generated certificate awarded on passing, ready for print or institutional submission.
  • Instructor Dashboard Ready — Reports are structured for easy review by instructors, enabling batch evaluation across cohorts.
