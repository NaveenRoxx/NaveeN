🔥 Product Spotlight

VR Welding Simulator

Immersive · Safe · Smart Training

A VR-based Welding Training Simulator designed to train students and operators in welding safety, machine setup, and welding technique — in a safe, cost-effective virtual environment. No gas, no sparks, no risk — just high-fidelity skill development from day one.

🥽 Designed for Meta Quest  |  Standalone VR  |  Hand Tracking + Controllers

Key Features

🦺

PPE & Safety Training

Mandatory PPE collection with guided voice instructions before entering the welding zone — building safe habits from the start.

⚠️

Hazard Identification

Interactive detection of workplace hazards throughout the environment, teaching trainees to recognise and respond to real-world risks.

🔍

Machine Defect Identification

Visual highlighting of machine defects with detailed safety explanations — teaching inspection skills before any operation begins.

⚙️

Complete Machine Setup

Step-by-step power control, amperage setting, polarity checking using a virtual multimeter, and proper welding gun and clamp connections.

🔧

Realistic Welding Simulation

Live welding experience with real-time analytics tracking welding speed, arc distance, and electrode angle for precise skill correction.

🏆

Performance Evaluation & Certificates

Automatic scoring system with generation of detailed reports and certificates — supports trainee name and roll number for institutional use.

🎙️

Voice Guidance

Step-by-step audio narration throughout every module, ensuring trainees can focus on the task without needing to read on-screen text.

🤝

Haptics & Sound Effects

Immersive haptic feedback and spatial audio that mimic the physical sensations and sounds of actual welding, deepening the learning experience.

🖐️

Controller & Hand Tracking

Full support for both Meta Quest controllers and natural hand-tracking — making the simulator accessible and comfortable for all trainees.

📊 Real-Time Welding Analytics

⚡ Welding Speed 📐 Arc Distance 🔄 Electrode Angle 🎯 Accuracy Score 📋 Auto Report 🏅 Certificate Generation
