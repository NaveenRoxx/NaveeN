About Me

Hi, I'm Naveen Kumar S — a Senior XR Developer at the Centre for Teaching, Learning & Virtual Skilling (CTLVS), IIT Kharagpur. I specialise in building immersive Unity experiences that bridge the physical and digital worlds.

From VR safety training for NTPC & L&T, maritime simulations for the Indian Navy, and defence training for IDEX (UAE), to e-learning WebGL modules published on the DIKSHA platform — I focus exclusively on Unity-based solutions that are both functionally robust and visually compelling.

My approach combines technical precision with creative innovation: advanced C# engineering, Shader Graph visuals, XR Interaction Toolkit, and cross-platform deployment across Meta Quest, HoloLens 2, HTC Vive, WebGL, Windows & Android.
